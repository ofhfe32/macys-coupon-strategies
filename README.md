## The short answer: what Macy’s coupon should you try first?

There is no single permanent Macy’s coupon code that works on every department. Macy’s official guidance says current offers are shown in the Shopping Bag and on its Deals & Promotions page. Only one promo code can be used per order, and the code may require a minimum purchase, eligible brands, or specific products.

A practical order of operations is:

1. Add the exact item to your Macy’s bag.
2. Check whether the product already has a sale, Limited-Time Special, or automatic offer.
3. Look in the Offers section for an offer Macy’s has attached to your account or cart.
4. Try the current code shown on the product or deal page, such as `BEST` when the item specifically displays it.

## How to use a Macy’s coupon online

The coupon field is located in the Shopping Bag and checkout flow rather than on every product page.

### Desktop steps

1. Add eligible products to your Macy’s Shopping Bag.
2. Open the bag and find the **Offers** section.
3. Select an available offer, or enter a code in the promo-code field.
4. Click **Apply**.
5. Confirm the discount appears in the order total.
6. Review shipping, exclusions, and the final subtotal before placing the order.

Macy’s official checkout instructions say shoppers can either choose an available offer or enter a promo code manually. The discount should appear before the order is completed.

### Mobile steps

The same basic process applies on mobile, although the Offers section may be collapsed. Expand the offer area in the Shopping Bag, enter the code, and wait for the order summary to refresh.

Do not rely on the product-page price alone. A product may show a promotional price before the code, then show a different price after the code is applied. The number that matters is the final eligible merchandise total.

## How to combine a Macy’s sale with a coupon

Macy’s sales generally fall into three groups:

- **Automatic markdowns**, where the reduced price appears without a code.
- **Code-required promotions**, where the product page instructs you to enter a code.
- **Limited-time offers**, which may have separate rules and may not accept an additional coupon.

The best approach is to test the code on one eligible item before building a large cart. This is especially useful when a promotion excludes brands such as designer labels, cosmetics, electronics, furniture, mattresses, or gift cards.

Macy’s describes “Everyday Value” items as products that are excluded from many promotions and coupons. Clearance and closeout items can also carry separate restrictions. A lower sticker price does not automatically mean the product will accept another discount.

When a product already shows a significant markdown, check these three prices:

1. The original or regular price.
2. The current sale price.
3. The final price after any eligible promo code.

A coupon can be technically valid but financially irrelevant if it does not reduce the final price further.

## Macy’s Money: useful, but it is not an instant coupon

Macy’s Money works differently from a normal promo code. You earn it during selected promotional periods and use it during a later redemption window.

Macy’s currently documents the following earning structure:

- Spend $50–$99.99 and earn $10.
- Spend $100–$149.99 and earn $20.
- Spend $150–$199.99 and earn $30.
- Spend $200 or more and earn $40.

The maximum is $40 in Macy’s Money per transaction. The earning and redemption dates are announced for each event, so these values do not mean that every Macy’s order automatically generates Macy’s Money.

This offer can make sense when you already expect to place another Macy’s order during the redemption period. It is less attractive if you are buying one item and have no realistic second purchase planned. A reward that expires before you use it is just a very decorative number in your account.

Macy’s Money can generally be redeemed on most merchandise, but restrictions apply. Gift cards, services, fees, and some other categories may be excluded. Always check the event-specific terms.

## Star Rewards: which level is worth paying attention to?

Bronze is the most accessible level because it is free to join and does not require a Macy’s Credit Card. Bronze members earn 1 point per eligible dollar and can receive member offers, birthday benefits, and selected shipping perks. Macy’s says every 1,000 points converts into $10 in Star Money, although exclusions and program conditions apply.

Silver, Gold, and Platinum are mainly relevant to shoppers who already use a Macy’s Card regularly:

- **Silver:** $1–$499.99 in annual qualifying Macy’s Card spend.
- **Gold:** $500–$1,199.99.
- **Platinum:** $1,200 or more.

The higher tiers can provide higher points earning and stronger shipping benefits. Macy’s lists free standard shipping at different thresholds depending on membership level and payment method. The general shipping page currently advertises free shipping with a $99 purchase for other shoppers, while certain Star Rewards cardholder levels may receive lower thresholds or no minimum under the relevant conditions.

Star Money is separate from a Macy’s coupon. You may be able to use it on a future purchase, but it has a 30-day validity period after issuance. It also cannot be used for gift cards, services, fees, and other excluded purchases.

## Free shipping and pickup can beat another coupon

Shipping is part of the discount calculation. Macy’s current shipping page advertises free shipping with a $99 purchase for general shoppers in the contiguous United States, with exclusions and an end date shown on the live offer page. It also promotes free in-store pickup for eligible orders.

Before adding an extra item just to reach the shipping threshold, compare:

- The cost of the added item.
- The shipping fee you would otherwise pay.
- Whether the item is returnable.
- Whether the additional item qualifies for the coupon or cashback.
- Whether the order would cross an exclusion category.

Free pickup can be the cleaner option when a nearby store has the product. It avoids delivery charges without requiring a larger cart.

## What to do when a Macy’s coupon does not work

Macy’s identifies several common reasons a code fails:

- The cart does not meet the minimum spend.
- The product or brand is excluded.
- The code has expired.
- Another promotion is already active.
- The code is limited to a particular channel.
- The item is sold by an excluded department or third party.

Try removing the code, refreshing the Shopping Bag, and checking the offer details again. If the product page displays a specific code, make sure the code is entered exactly as shown. A space at the end can be enough to make a valid code look broken.

If you forgot to use an offer, Macy’s says you may be able to request a price adjustment for an order placed within the previous 10 days, subject to eligibility rules. That is not automatic, so submit the request promptly and keep the order information available.

For ShopBack users, avoid placing a second order immediately after a failed code attempt without restarting the ShopBack session. If the first visit was not tracked correctly, the next purchase may also fail to record cashback.
